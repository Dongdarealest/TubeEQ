Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Short version

TubeEQ collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads. Your settings never leave your browser.

What the extension stores

  • Your EQ settings, presets, per-channel preferences and UI language, stored locally in your browser via chrome.storage (synced to your own Google account if you use Chrome Sync).
  • Nothing else. No browsing history, no video titles, no personal data.

What the extension sends

  • Nothing. TubeEQ makes no network requests of its own. It only reads the YouTube page to attach its audio processing.

Permissions

  • youtube.com, the only host permission, required to process audio on YouTube pages.
  • storage, required to save your settings in your browser.

Open source

TubeEQ is MIT licensed. You can audit every line at github.com/Dongdarealest/TubeEQ.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub.
